**How login should work after promotion:** The role is stored once in the database on the User record. When a teacher gets promoted to Admin, that same field changes from `"teacher"` to `"admin"`. So next time they log in, the system checks the database role — not what they pick on the dropdown — and sends them to `/admin`. The Role dropdown on Sign In is just a visual label; the real role always comes from the database. So a promoted teacher will now log in and land on the **Admin dashboard automatically**, not the Teacher one.
